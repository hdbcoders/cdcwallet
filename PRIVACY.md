# Privacy Policy for CDC Wallet

**Effective date:** 14 Aug 2026

## Overview

CDC Wallet ("the App") is a personal bookmark manager for Singapore Government voucher links (such as RedeemSG / CDC Vouchers, SG60, and Climate Vouchers). It is **not an official Singapore Government or CDC application** and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Government of Singapore.

This policy explains what information the App handles, where it is stored, and your choices. In short: **the App stores only what you put into it, on your device, and does not collect, transmit, or share any data with the developer or any third party.**

## Information the App stores

- **Voucher links** - the URLs and tokens of government voucher pages that you add, paste, or share into the App.
- **Voucher details** - the information shown on those pages (campaign name, categories, remaining balances, expiry dates), read on your device when you open or refresh a voucher.
- **App preferences** - your display choices (theme, language, text size, collapsed or expanded summary) and your encrypted backup files (if you create one).

All of this data is **yours and stays on your device**. Nothing is uploaded to any server operated by the developer.

## How the data is protected

- **Encryption at rest** - your voucher data is stored in an encrypted database whose key is held in the device's hardware-backed key store.
- **No cloud backup** - Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of app data are explicitly disabled.
- **Encrypted backups (your choice)** - if you export a backup file, it is encrypted with a password you choose. The developer cannot decrypt it; if you lose that password, the backup cannot be recovered.
- **Secure connections** - all network traffic is restricted to secure (HTTPS) connections; unsecured traffic is disabled.

## When the App uses the Internet

The App has **no servers and no analytics**. Internet access occurs only when **you** take an action that loads the RedeemSG website inside the App's browser view (for example, tapping a voucher to open or refresh it). Those requests go directly between your device and the RedeemSG service; the App does not observe, log, or relay them.

## Permissions the App requests

- **Internet** - used solely to load voucher pages you choose to open.
- **Storage write (Android 9 and earlier only)** - used only to save the backup file you explicitly export.

## Clipboard

The App reads the clipboard **only when you tap the "paste" action** on the add-voucher screen. It never reads the clipboard automatically or in the background.

## Children's privacy

The App does not target children and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

## Data deletion

You can delete any voucher from the App at any time. Deleting all vouchers and uninstalling the App removes all data stored by the App from your device. Backup files you exported are outside the App's control once created; delete them yourself if you no longer want them.
