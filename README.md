# CDC Wallet

CDC Wallet is a simple, private app that keeps all your Singapore Government voucher links in one safe place. CDC Vouchers, SG60, and Climate Vouchers links arrive by SMS, email, or WhatsApp, and are easy to lose. CDC Wallet stores them for you, remembers how much is left on each one, and reminds you before they expire.

> **Not an official app.** CDC Wallet is a personal project and is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to** the Singapore Government or CDC. It is a bookmarks app: it only saves and opens your voucher links. The actual voucher pages are run by the Government, and the app never spends or redeems anything for you.

## Features

- **Save a voucher in two taps** - paste the link or share it straight from another app. The app lets you know if you have already saved it.
- **See how much is left** - open any voucher and see your remaining balance, split by category (Supermarket, Heartland, Climate, and more).
- **Refresh anytime** - tap a voucher to open the real page and update its balances. Nothing ever updates on its own in the background.
- **Never miss an expiry** - each voucher shows how many days are left, with a clear warning when it is running out.
- **Tidy up** - archive vouchers you no longer need, bring them back later, or delete them for good.
- **Move to a new phone** - export an encrypted backup file and import it on your next device. Only you can open it, with a password you choose.
- **In your language** - English, 中文, Bahasa Melayu, and தமிழ்.
- **Easy on the eyes** - light and dark themes, bigger text sizes for comfortable reading, and dyslexia-friendly fonts (Atkinson Hyperlegible or OpenDyslexic) from the Accessibility menu.
- **Always up to date** - the app quietly checks the Play Store for new versions (at most once a day); when an update is available, a badge appears in the menu and a single tap takes you to the Play Store.

## Privacy

Your privacy is built into the app. No account, no servers, no analytics, and nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to export a backup. All voucher data is stored on your device in an encrypted database.

Read the full [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/hdbcoders/cdcwallet/blob/main/PRIVACY.md) for details.

## Installing

CDC Wallet is available on the Google Play Store for Android phones running Android 7.0 or newer.
